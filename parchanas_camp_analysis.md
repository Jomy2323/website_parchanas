### 3. School Programs (Programas Escolares)

**Overview:** "We propose to your students a different complementary learning experience. The Environmental Studies Center of Alcácer do Sal - Herdade das Parchanas, adapts and personalizes activity programs according to your needs and expectations, as well as those of your students."

#### Sport and Adventure Programs
- One day of sport and adventure
- Two days and one night
- Three days and two nights

#### Pedagogical Programs

**1. Applied Mathematics in the Field (Matemática Aplicada no Campo)**
- Group walking route
- Students face field problems requiring math knowledge
- Topics: volume determination, number divisibility, triangle similarity, equations, functions, probabilities

**2. Renewable Energy (Energias Renováveis)**
- Aligned with European 2020 objective
- Focus on alternative energies
- Topics: Mechanical to Electrical Energy conversion, Electromagnetic Induction, Biodiesel, Biogas, etc.

**3. Forest and Natural Resources (Floresta e Recursos Naturais)**
- Learn to use the forest
- Understand importance of preservation

**4. Stork Nesting (Nidificação das Cegonhas)**
- Topics: nesting, migration, feeding, habitat
- Importance of bird preservation
- Includes film screening to foster interest and curiosity

**5. A Day in the Countryside (Um Dia no Campo)**
- Depicts life in the countryside
- Students participate directly in agricultural activities:
  - Animal care
  - Bread making
  - Planting and caring for fruit trees
- Provides integrated vision of food origin and production processes

**6. Environmental Camping (Acantonamento Ambiental)**
- With overnight stays in dormitories

**7. The Olive (Olive Oil Production Cycle) - A Azeitona**
- Takes place in October and November
- Depicts olive oil production cycle
- Condition of olive trees

## History

### Foundation (1989)
"It was in 1989 that we took the first steps to become what we are today, a second home for so many young people."

### Initial Phase
- Started as Environmental Studies Center of Alcácer do Sal
- Based on learning about nature and environment
- **First year:** Only school field trips
- **Focus:** Learning about Stork Nesting through direct observation, explanatory videos, and classes

### Development
**Second year:** Introduced other nature and environment themes
- Provided fun moments for students to acquire and consolidate knowledge
- Activities included:
  - Olive picking and oil making
  - Herbarium making
  - Fishing rod making
  - Perfume making from plants
  - Observing living beings and animals
  - Feeding animals

### Expansion
**Following years:**
- Started other programs with new themes and activities
- Inaugurated Graduate Trips for schools
- Eventually started Summer Camps program (Colónias de Férias)

### Early Days
"In an initial phase, campers slept in our tents, which were in the courtyard."

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I book more than one week?**
> A: Yes. As long as there are vacancies, there is no limit to the number of weeks you can participate.

**Q: I have dietary restrictions, do I have to bring my own food?**
> A: In case of dietary restrictions or food allergies, you should inform our team at the time of booking, so we can adapt your meals, so it is not necessary to bring from home.

**Q: I take medication daily. What do I do at Parchanas?**
> A: If you take medication regularly (or SOS), inform our team of your therapeutic plan. Medications are kept by the monitors responsible for each family and their administration is ensured by them.

**Q: Is it possible to bring souvenirs?**
> A: Yes, at the end of each week we have numerous mementos available that you can take home. We have photos of the activities (delivered on a USB stick), t-shirts and sweatshirts, bracelets and much more...!

## Summary

Herdade das Parchanas is a comprehensive environmental education center and summer camp located in the heart of the Alentejo region in Portugal. Since 1989, it has evolved from a simple environmental studies center focused on stork nesting education to a multi-faceted camp offering summer programs, team building activities, and school educational programs.

The camp emphasizes four core values: autonomy and self-confidence, friendship and sharing, companionship and empathy, and creativity and respect. Set in a pristine natural environment along the Sado River, surrounded by 50 hectares of rice fields and Mediterranean habitat, it offers children ages 6-17 and adult groups an immersive experience combining adventure activities, environmental education, and personal development.

With capacity for up to 110 participants per week, extensive facilities including dormitories, sports fields, a swimming pool, stables, and specialized activity areas, Parchanas provides a safe, structured, and enriching environment. The camp operates year-round during Christmas, Easter, and Summer breaks, with flexible weekly or bi-weekly programs.

From canoeing and climbing to bread-making and animal care, from traditional games to specialized educational programs in mathematics, renewable energy, and forest conservation, Parchanas offers a truly unique "second home" experience that creates lasting memories, friendships, and personal growth for all participants.
